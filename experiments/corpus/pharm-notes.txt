Pharmacology Study Notes: Antimicrobials

Beta-lactam antibiotics require an intact beta-lactam ring. Penicillins are
destroyed by penicillinase, so isoxazolyl penicillins such as oxacillin are used
against Staphylococcus aureus. There is no cross reactivity between the
penicillins and the cephalosporins, so a penicillin allergy does not imply a
cephalosporin allergy.

Aminoglycosides are concentration dependent killers. They require a high peak
to achieve killing, and they are ineffective against anaerobes because uptake
depends on oxygen dependent transport. They are ototoxic and nephrotoxic, so
monitoring of both drug level and renal function is required.

Vancomycin must be infused slowly over at least one hour to avoid red man
syndrome, which is a histamine mediated reaction rather than true allergy.
Trough levels should be checked for renally impaired patients.

Fluoroquinolones inhibit DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV. They are associated
with tendinitis, peripheral neuropathy and aortic aneurysm, so they are now
reserved for infections with no safer alternative.
