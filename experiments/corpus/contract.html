Master Services Agreement

This agreement is made between the Client and the Supplier and takes effect on the date of last signature.

1. Definitions

"Deliverable" means any output identified as a deliverable in a statement of work. "Acceptance" means the Client confirming in writing that a Deliverable meets the acceptance criteria.

2. Term and Termination

This agreement continues for an initial term of thirty six months. Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety days written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that remains uncured after thirty days notice.

3. Limitation of Liability

The total aggregate liability of either party is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim, except for liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and for breach of confidentiality, which is uncapped.

4. Payment Terms

Invoices are payable within forty five days of receipt. The Client may withhold payment for Deliverables under dispute while the parties negotiate.
